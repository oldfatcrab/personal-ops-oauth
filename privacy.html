PERSONAL OPS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Personal Ops is a personal-use application operated by its developer for the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, Personal Ops accesses Google Tasks lists and tasks. This can include task titles, notes, due dates, completion status, and related identifiers. Its Google Tasks permission allows reading and changing tasks and task lists.

How data is used

The application uses Google Tasks information only to carry out the account owner's personal task-management workflows, including owner-configured automations. These workflows may read, create, update, complete, or delete tasks.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are kept locally using macOS Keychain. Task information may be processed by local scripts and may appear in notes or logs produced by the owner's workflows. Personal Ops does not operate a public service collecting other users' task data.

If the owner chooses to connect an AI or other cloud service to a workflow, relevant task information may be transmitted to that service to perform the requested operation, subject to that service's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Sharing and advertising

Google Tasks data is not sold, and Personal Ops does not use it for targeted advertising. Data is shared with other services only as part of workflows configured by the account owner.

Control and revocation

The account owner can review or delete tasks in Google Tasks and revoke the application's Google access from Google Account connections. Revoking access stops new API access but does not automatically erase copies already created in local notes, logs, or connected services.

Contact

For inquiries about this personal application, use the project's GitHub issue tracker.
