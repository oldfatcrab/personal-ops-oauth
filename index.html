Personal Ops

A small, privately operated toolset for managing the developer's Google Tasks.

About this application

Personal Ops connects to the Google Tasks API with the account owner's permission. It helps the owner read, organize, create, update, complete, and delete tasks as part of personal productivity workflows.

This application is for its developer's own use. It is not a public product and does not offer sign-ups or accounts to other users.

Your data

Google Tasks access is used for the owner's task-management workflows. For details about data access, processing, and revoking permission, see the Privacy Policy.
